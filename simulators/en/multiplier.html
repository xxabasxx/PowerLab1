Note: This circuit is a cascaded doubler. The upper column (capacitors Ca) swings with the AC source, while the lower column (capacitors Cb) raises the DC voltage step by step. The odd diodes clamp the upper nodes during the negative half-cycle and charge the AC-column capacitors; the even diodes pump charge into the DC column during the positive half-cycle. Each stage adds about 2Vp to the output, so for N stages the output equals 2N·Vp − 2N·VD. The internal output resistance grows with the cube of N, therefore with a heavy load or many stages the voltage drop and ripple increase dramatically.
