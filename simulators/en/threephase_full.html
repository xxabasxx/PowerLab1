Note: A 3-phase bridge rectifier utilizes 6 diodes. At any instant, two diodes conduct: one connected to the phase with the highest voltage (for DC+) and the other connected to the phase with the lowest voltage (for DC-). This increases the ripple frequency to 6 times the input frequency (300Hz), producing an exceptionally smooth DC output.
