Note: In a 3-phase half-wave (3-pulse) rectifier, three sources operate with a 120° phase shift. At any moment, the diode connected to the phase with the highest positive voltage conducts. As a result, the output voltage never drops to zero, and the ripple frequency is 3 times the input frequency.
